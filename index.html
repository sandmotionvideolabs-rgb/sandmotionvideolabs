Questions

Good Questions, Straight Answers.

How much does a video cost?

It depends on length, style and how much is created from scratch. Tell us your idea and we send a clear quote, free and with no obligation.

How long does a project take?

Short social videos can often be turned around quickly. Longer or animated projects take more time. We confirm a timeline in your quote.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. We work remotely with clients worldwide and share drafts through online review links.

How do revisions work?

Every project includes a set number of revision rounds, agreed before we start, so there are no surprises.

What do I need to provide?

Just your idea. If you have footage, brand files or references, send them along. If not, we can help plan it.

Can you handle bigger projects?

Yes. We work with individual creators, small businesses, growing brands and agencies. Larger or ongoing projects are scoped together in a short call.

5 Videos Every Small Business Should Make First (And What Each One Should Say)

16 November 2026 · 7 min read · By Sandmotion Video Labs

In this guide

  • Start with one video that matches your goal, not five at once.
  • Each video has one job: trust, leads, sales or teaching.
  • One good video can be cut into several short clips.

You know video works. But you can't make everything at once, and a wasted first video is a wasted budget. The smartest small businesses start with one video that does one job, then grow from there. Here are the five worth making, in the order that makes sense for most.

The 5 videos, and what each one is for

  1. The "how we help" explainer (60 to 90 seconds). For leads. Problem, solution, next step. Put it on your website and your Google Business Profile.
  2. The customer story or "meet the team" video. For trust. People buy from people they believe. A real face and a real result do more than any slogan.
  3. The product demo or offer video. For sales. Show the product working, name one benefit, then give the offer.
  4. A set of short Reels and Shorts. For reach. Cut from the videos above, they bring new people to your page.
  5. A simple promotional video for ads. For paid reach, once you know which message works. Test organically first.
Start with the goal, not the format. If you need enquiries, make the explainer. If you need trust, make the customer story. The picker above chooses for you.

What every small business video should say

  • Who it is for. Name the customer in the first line.
  • One problem and one solution. Not ten.
  • Proof. A real customer, result or photo of your work.
  • One next step. Call, book, visit or message. Say it, and show it on screen.

Mistakes that waste a first video

  • Trying to say everything in one video.
  • No captions, even though many people watch without sound.
  • A long introduction before the point.
  • No call to action at the end.
  • Making a long film when a short clip would do.

How to make it affordable

Film simple clips on a phone, in good light, with clear sound. Then pay for what a professional does best: story, editing, captions, sound and polish. One strong video, cut into several clips, goes much further than five rushed ones.

Frequently asked questions

Which video should a small business make first?

The one that matches your biggest goal. For enquiries, a short explainer. For trust, a customer story. For sales, a product demo. The picker on this page recommends one for you.

How long should a small business video be?

Usually 30 to 90 seconds. Make it as short as the message allows.

Do I need to film it myself?

Many businesses film short clips on a phone and send them for editing. If you want help planning or shooting, say so and we will quote it separately.

Can one video become several?

Yes. A single video can be cut into several short clips for Reels and Shorts.

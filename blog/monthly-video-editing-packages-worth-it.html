How monthly packages work

You agree a set number of videos each month, with a clear list of what is included. Published 2026 guides describe typical plans, such as around four long videos plus a dozen short clips a month, or eight long videos for busier channels. Prices vary widely by editor level and scope.

What a good package lists in writing

  • The number of long videos and short clips each month.
  • Extras: thumbnails, captions, motion graphics, music.
  • Revision rounds per video.
  • Turnaround time and a delivery schedule.
  • Ownership of final files and project files.
  • How to pause, change or end the plan.

When a monthly plan wins

  • You publish at least weekly.
  • You are tired of re-briefing new people.
  • You want a consistent look across videos.
  • You need a predictable monthly cost.

When per-video is better

  • You publish rarely or in bursts.
  • You are testing an idea.
  • You only need one video this quarter.
Watch for scope creep: one guide describes a retainer for four long videos plus about twenty Shorts that also asked for a share of ad revenue. Read what a flat fee actually covers.

Frequently asked questions

Are monthly editing packages cheaper?

Often per video, once you publish regularly, and always easier to predict. The real saving is your time.

What if I need more videos one month?

Agree this upfront. Good plans allow extra videos at a stated price.

Can I cancel?

Check the notice period and how to pause or end the plan before you start.

What is usually included?

Editing, captions, a set number of revisions and an agreed delivery schedule. Extras are listed separately.

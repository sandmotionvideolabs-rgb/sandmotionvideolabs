The short answer

For editing alone, published 2026 price lists put one Reel at roughly ₹500 to ₹2,500, depending on the editor and what is included. If you want scripting, a shoot and a strategy, the price rises quickly. Here is the picture from several published sources:

Are Shorts priced differently from Reels?

Usually not. Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and Facebook Reels are all short vertical videos, so editors price them the same way. Better still, one edit can often be published on all three, which lowers your cost per view. Ask your editor to deliver files ready for each platform.

What you are really paying for

A good Reel is not just a trimmed clip. You are paying for:

  1. A strong first second. The hook decides whether people keep watching.
  2. Pacing and cuts that match how short videos are watched.
  3. Captions. Many people watch with the sound off.
  4. Sound and music that suit your brand.
  5. A consistent look across every video, so your page feels like yours.
  6. Revisions so the final result is right.

The cheapest option often covers the first line of this list only. That is fine for casual use, but weak for a business that wants Reels to bring customers.

How many Reels do you need?

Published packages are commonly built around 4, 8, 12 or 16 Reels a month. The right number is the one you can keep up and afford to do well. Four strong, consistent Reels a month beat twenty rushed ones, and you can always scale up once you see what works.

Where to put your money, by type of business

Personal brands, coaches and educators

You are the product, so keep your face and voice central. Film simple talking clips and invest in editing, captions and a recognisable style. Cutting several Reels from one longer recording keeps costs down.

Local shops, salons, restaurants and clinics

Short, real clips of your work, products or team often beat polished adverts. A few good Reels a month with captions and a clear call to action, such as "call", "visit" or "book", is a sensible start.

Online stores

Product demonstrations, unboxings and before-and-after clips do well. Plan for several versions of the same product video, and check whether you want some for paid ads, which cost more to produce.

Startups and agencies

You need volume, consistency and quick turnaround. A monthly package with a clear list of deliverables, plus a shared review process, saves time. Larger or ongoing needs are best scoped in a short call.

Mistakes and hidden costs to avoid

  • Paying per Reel without knowing what is included. Ask about captions, music, revisions and delivery time.
  • Choosing only on price. A ₹500 edit with no hook or captions rarely helps your business.
  • Promises of "going viral". Nobody can guarantee views. Be wary of any package that does.
  • Music problems. Business accounts can face limits on popular tracks, so ask your editor to use music you are allowed to use.
  • Unclear ownership. Make sure you receive the final files and know how you may use them, including in ads.
  • Starting without a plan. Random Reels cost the same as planned ones and work worse.
Good to know: producing a Reel specifically as an advertisement costs far more than an organic Reel. One agency guide puts ad production in India at about ₹30,000 to ₹1,50,000 per ad. Start with organic Reels and promote the winners.

7 ways to get more from your Reels budget

  1. Batch your filming. Film several clips in one session.
  2. Repurpose. Cut a longer video into several Reels.
  3. Use one style. A fixed caption and colour style saves time and builds recognition.
  4. Send a clear brief. Say who the Reel is for and what you want people to do next.
  5. Agree revision rounds. Two clear rounds beat endless tweaks.
  6. Start small. Test four Reels before committing to a larger package.
  7. Track results. Keep what works and drop what doesn't.

Frequently asked questions

How much does one Instagram Reel cost to edit in India?

Published 2026 price lists show a wide spread: around ₹500 for a basic edit at low-cost studios, about ₹800 to ₹2,500 from mid-level editors, and more for senior editors or reels with motion graphics. What is included matters as much as the price.

Are Reels and YouTube Shorts priced differently?

Usually not. They are both short vertical videos, so most editors price them the same. Cutting one video for both platforms often costs little extra.

How many Reels should a small business post each month?

Published packages are commonly built around 4, 8, 12 or 16 reels a month. The right number is the one you can keep up consistently and afford to do well.

What is included in a Reels package?

Typically editing, captions, branding and a set number of revision rounds. Some packages also include a script and concept, and fuller ones include a shoot. Always ask for the list in writing.

Do I need to shoot the footage myself?

Not always. Many businesses film short clips on a phone and send them for editing. If you want help planning or shooting, say so and we will quote it separately.

Can I start with just a few Reels?

Yes. A small trial batch is a low-risk way to see if the style and the working relationship suit you before you commit to a monthly plan.
